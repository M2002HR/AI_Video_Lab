# P0003 Source Prompt Analysis v01

## Reusable creative DNA
- strict 10-second timeline;
- fixed 90-degree overhead camera for the full clip;
- no tilt, rotation, push, pan or other camera movement;
- ultra-realistic premium food-commercial treatment;
- soft diffused studio light;
- pure white minimalist tabletop and centered composition;
- invisible-chef mechanism with no visible hands, arms or body;
- realistic gravity, collisions, settling, liquid/material behavior and food motion;
- rapid preparation-to-finished-product transformation;
- final hero presentation followed by one lifted piece and a bite reveal.

## KEEP
- 10-second duration;
- 16:9 aspect ratio;
- fixed exactly top-down camera;
- clean white studio background;
- centered uncluttered composition;
- invisible-chef rule;
- timecoded sequential action grammar;
- realistic physical behavior;
- final product hero shot;
- lifted-piece / bite-ending motif, adapted to a truffle.

## ADAPT
- brownie preparation -> handmade chocolate-truffle preparation and gift-box assembly;
- round cake pan -> shallow open natural kraft gift box plus dark fluted paper candy cups;
- brownie batter -> glossy dark-chocolate ganache/truffle mixture that becomes individual handmade centers;
- flour/sieve/baking workflow -> forming, coating and seating truffles into paper cups;
- chocolate chunks topping batter -> colorful coating particles/nonpareils/sprinkles applied to individual truffles;
- brownie slicing -> no cutting; instead reveal a completed tightly packed assortment;
- serving plate -> final kraft gift box presentation;
- lifted brownie slice -> one multicolor truffle lifting from its cup, then a natural bite revealing dark chocolate interior;
- source composition instruction -> preserve the real box language, assortment density and broad top-down arrangement while replacing the source wooden tabletop with a pure white studio surface.

## REMOVE
- cake pan and parchment workflow;
- butter/sugar/eggs/flour/cocoa/baking-powder steps that only describe brownie batter;
- brownie baking/rising transformation;
- knife and equal-slice cutting;
- white serving plate and circular brownie-slice arrangement;
- brownie-specific crust/crumb structure;
- any watermark, text, logo or branding visible in the product photograph.

## Product leakage / contradiction checks
1. Source template says brownie; P0003 must never output brownies, cake slices, cake pops or baked-cake geometry.
2. Source template asks for an exact reference presentation; the real truffle photograph is product authority, but its wooden table and watermark are explicitly non-authoritative.
3. Source template is extremely dense for 10 seconds. Truffle adaptation must group related actions and avoid too many utensil changes.
4. The real assortment contains many individual pieces, which creates count/layout drift risk. Exact count should not be asserted unless a later reference/continuity gate supports it reliably.
5. Procedurally perfect spheres would violate the handmade product identity. Natural small variations are required.
6. Levitation must feel physically weighted and controlled, not supernatural with frictionless motion.

## Tool-specific assumptions
- The source was presented as a Gemini prompt, but exact model/version is not yet known.
- Any final model-specific syntax, reference-stack order or negative constraints are deferred until the final prompt/preflight stage.

## Adaptation rule
Preserve the source camera, invisible-chef mechanism, clean studio grammar, realistic physics and compressed timecoded transformation structure. Replace all brownie-specific content with identity-faithful truffle formation, coating and kraft-box assembly behavior.

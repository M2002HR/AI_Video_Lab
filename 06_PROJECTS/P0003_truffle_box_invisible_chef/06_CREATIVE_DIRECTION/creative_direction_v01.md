# P0003 Creative Direction v01 — Locked

## Campaign idea
A premium 10-second invisible-chef transformation in which raw chocolate-truffle components self-organize, coat, and assemble into the exact colorful gift-box presentation defined by the approved product references.

## Product priority
The truffle assortment is the hero. The mechanism is entertaining but must never overpower or redesign the product. The final full-box presentation must read as a clean commercial reveal of the real product family.

## Visual language
- ultra-realistic premium food commercial;
- fixed exactly 90-degree top-down camera for the entire clip;
- one continuous 16:9 shot with no camera movement, tilt, zoom, pan, roll, or rotation;
- seamless pure white matte studio tabletop;
- centered, minimal workspace;
- soft diffused studio lighting with realistic contact shadows;
- realistic materials and believable weight, collisions, settling, and granular sprinkle behavior.

## Creative mechanism
Invisible chef / self-moving objects. No hands, arms, body, face, sleeves, shadows of a person, or implied visible operator. Utensils and ingredients move as though manipulated by a physically plausible invisible person.

## Rhythm
Fast, readable transformation beats. Favor fewer clear actions over excessive utensil changes. Motion should accelerate and decelerate naturally rather than teleport.

## Product construction logic
The clip may simplify real confectionery production for a 10-second commercial, but it should preserve a believable sequence:
1. box and paper cups prepare themselves;
2. dark chocolate centers form;
3. centers receive the real coating families;
4. coated truffles assemble into the box;
5. full hero assortment settles;
6. one truffle lifts and receives the source-template bite gag.

## Final-presentation lock
The complete box must resolve toward `REF-P0003-001` for clean composition and toward the original photograph for ultimate product identity. Preserve 25-piece dense assortment grammar, kraft packaging, dark cups, varied coating families, and handmade irregularity.

## Forbidden drift
- no brownies, cake slices, cake pops, sticks, cupcakes, cookies, macarons, bonbons, or unrelated foods;
- no redesigned luxury packaging;
- no text, watermark, label, or invented brand;
- no wooden tabletop;
- no visible human anatomy;
- no cinematic camera movement;
- no magical sparkles, fantasy particles, or weightless motion.

## Gate
Creative Direction: **PASS / LOCKED**.

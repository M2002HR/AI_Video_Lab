# P0003 Product Identity v01

## Source authority
Primary authority: `SESSION-ASSET-P0003-IMG-001`, the current uploaded real product photograph.

## Category
Handmade chocolate truffle assortment / gift-box confectionery.

## Core geometry and proportions
- Individual pieces are small, near-spherical truffles rather than perfectly geometric bonbons.
- Natural handmade variation exists in diameter, roundness, seating angle and coating thickness.
- Truffles sit individually in compact dark fluted paper candy cups.
- The box is shallow, open, natural kraft-brown cardboard with a simple folded construction.
- The assortment is densely packed in a roughly square/diamond top-down presentation.

## Surface and material
- Truffle surfaces are densely covered with edible decorations.
- Two important coating families are visible: tiny round nonpareils and thin elongated sprinkles/jimmies.
- Coating particles are matte-to-satin and create a granular, highly textured exterior.
- Dark chocolate is the expected interior material; it should read as rich, dense and edible rather than plastic.
- Paper cups are dark charcoal/gray, matte and visibly fluted.
- Kraft box is uncoated natural cardboard, not glossy luxury packaging.

## Color family
The assortment contains varied combinations including:
- white;
- pink;
- bright/dark blue;
- purple;
- yellow/lime;
- red/green/white;
- orange/brown;
- mixed rainbow combinations.

Both monochrome and mixed-color coatings are present.

## Packaging/components
- one shallow open kraft box;
- individual dark fluted paper cups;
- tightly packed colorful truffles;
- no visible lid in the authoritative presentation;
- no ribbon, plastic window, metallic insert, branded sleeve or luxury redesign.

## Critical identity details
1. Handmade, slightly irregular truffle geometry.
2. Dense granular sprinkle/nonpareil coating.
3. Clear diversity of colors and coating types.
4. One truffle per dark fluted cup.
5. Natural kraft box with simple construction.
6. Dense gift-box assortment presentation.

## Natural imperfections to preserve
- subtle variation in roundness;
- slightly uneven particle distribution;
- small differences in size;
- slight seating-angle differences;
- imperfect but appetizing handmade finish.

## Scene contamination — not identity
- rustic wooden tabletop;
- visible `CHOCOLAT.CHOCOLATE` watermark/text;
- exact lighting/color cast of the source photograph;
- any implied brand identity from the watermark.

## Uncertainty
The single photograph does not expose a bitten/cut interior, so dark-chocolate interior is a conservative product-category inference to be shown naturally only if the later creative direction keeps the bite ending. Interior color/texture must not become elaborate or filled unless further evidence is supplied.

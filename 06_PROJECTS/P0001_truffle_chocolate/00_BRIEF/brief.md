# P0001 Brief — Colorful Chocolate Truffle Miniature Commercial

## Minimum viable inputs received
- Product: handmade colorful chocolate truffles in an open natural kraft box.
- Source/template prompt: 10-second miniature-chef chocolate-cherry-cheesecake commercial supplied as an image.
- Creative/style reference: storyboard/reference image showing tiny red-uniform chefs working around a giant dessert.

## Deliverable v1
- Type: short AI product advertisement.
- Duration: 10 seconds.
- Aspect ratio: 16:9.
- Target: Google Flow, Gemini Omni Flash, Ingredients/References to Video.
- Visual target: premium photorealistic macro miniature-food commercial.
- Core creative device: tiny chocolatiers working around oversized handmade truffles, ending on a clean hero reveal of the truffle assortment in its kraft box.

## Primary success criteria
1. Product remains unmistakably chocolate truffles, not cake pops, bonbons, cupcakes, cookies, donuts or cheesecake.
2. Final chocolates strongly resemble the supplied product: handmade near-spherical forms, colorful nonpareils or elongated sprinkles, dark fluted paper cups, open kraft box, dense assortment.
3. Product identity remains stable through the entire video.
4. Miniature characters and actions remain physically coherent and do not overwhelm the product.
5. Camera, lighting, scale and environment feel like premium real macro commercial photography.
6. Final 1–2 seconds provide a stable hero frame suitable for branding in post.

## Current assumptions
- No text/logo needs to be generated in-model.
- The visible `CHOCOLAT.CHOCOLATE` watermark in the source photo is not product identity and must not be reproduced.
- Old cheesecake/cherry content is reference DNA only and must not contaminate the new product.

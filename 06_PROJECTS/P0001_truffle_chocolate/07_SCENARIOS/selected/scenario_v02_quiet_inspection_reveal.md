# Selected Scenario v02 — Quiet Inspection → Full Box Reveal

status: selected_for_v2  
project: P0001  
duration: 10s  
format: 16:9  
target: Google Flow / Gemini Omni Flash

## Why v02 exists
KF01 v01 evidence (R0011–R0013) showed that even one requested brush encouraged extra tools, bowls, loose ingredients and generic workshop staging. The opening scene also became detached from the kraft box, making the planned continuous pull-back physically inconsistent.

v02 removes all tools and puts the opening action inside the actual kraft box from frame one.

## Core idea
A premium continuous miniature-confectionery shot begins close on one real-looking multicolor hero truffle already seated in its dark fluted cup **inside the real kraft gift box**. The three recurring tiny chocolatiers stand on visible kraft/cardboard space between nearby cups and perform only subtle inspection gestures with empty hands. The camera then pulls smoothly backward and slightly upward, revealing neighboring truffles and eventually the complete box.

## Story beats
### Beat A — 00:00–00:02.2 — Quiet inspection
- One hero multicolor truffle dominates the local box area.
- Exactly three recurring chefs are tiny relative to it; all three faces remain readable.
- Hands are empty.
- Character A leans/looks toward the truffle.
- Character B makes one restrained open-hand inspection gesture.
- Character C watches with a tiny nod or hand movement.
- No props, no loose ingredients, no product manipulation.

### Beat B — 00:02.2–00:06.2 — Assortment expansion
- One continuous camera pull-back and shallow rise reveals neighboring pink, blue, purple, white and elongated-sprinkle truffles already present in their cups.
- The same kraft box interior becomes increasingly readable.
- Chefs remain near the original hero-truffle region and do not walk across the scene.

### Beat C — 00:06.2–00:09.0 — Full-box reveal
- Camera continues in the same direction until the natural kraft gift box and colorful assortment read as the main product.
- Product identity stays stable; no count/morph changes are introduced.

### Beat D — 00:09.0–00:10.0 — Hero hold
- Camera eases close to a stop.
- Character motion settles.
- Final frame is product-dominant and clean for post-production branding.

## Scale rule
Target one chef's full standing height at roughly one-third of the hero truffle diameter. Equivalent simple cue: **hero truffle diameter ≈ 3× chef height**. Scale must remain constant during the clip.

## Character behavior
- exactly the recurring three chefs from R0010;
- empty hands throughout v2;
- no brush, bowl, tray, ladder, cart, utensil or ingredient;
- no back-facing identity loss in the opening frame;
- no long-distance walking;
- only restrained inspection gestures.

## Physical continuity
The opening scene and final reveal are the same physical box. The hero truffle is not isolated on a separate tabletop. The camera simply reveals more of the already-existing arrangement as it moves backward/upward.

## Camera
One movement only: continuous slow pull-back + shallow upward arc. No cuts, orbit reversal, whip pan, sudden zoom or scene transformation.

## Final frame
Premium 3/4 view of the natural kraft box filled with colorful handmade truffles. Tiny chefs remain visible only as a scale/story accent.

## Risk level
Lower than v01 because all active tools and loose ingredient interactions have been removed.

# Selected Scenario v01 — Final Touch → Full Box Reveal

status: selected_for_v1  
project: P0001  
duration: 10s  
format: 16:9  
target: Google Flow / Gemini Omni Flash

## Core idea
A premium continuous miniature-confectionery shot begins extremely close on one real-looking colorful hero truffle. The three recurring tiny chocolatiers perform only minimal finishing/inspection motions around that truffle. The camera then performs one continuous smooth pull-back along a shallow upward arc, revealing more of the colorful assortment and finally the complete kraft gift box as a clean hero product shot.

The commercial communicates `artisan final inspection / finishing` rather than attempting to show the entire manufacturing process. This deliberately reduces object manipulation, teleportation, morphing and continuity risk while preserving the tiny-worker creative DNA of the supplied template.

## Why this scenario is selected
- product remains physically stable throughout;
- no truffle needs to transform, appear, disappear or change coating;
- no character needs to carry a truffle;
- exact three-character cast can remain in one local area;
- only one simple tool is needed at most;
- camera movement itself creates visual progression;
- the scene naturally transitions from texture/detail evidence to full assortment and packaging reveal;
- final frame is useful as a commercial hero shot;
- compatible with existing reference hierarchy.

## Story beats
### Beat A — 00:00–00:02.5 — Macro final touch
Start on one representative multicolor round-nonpareil truffle in its dark fluted paper cup. The three tiny chocolatiers are clearly miniature relative to the truffle. Character A performs one short, gentle finishing pass with a tiny soft confectionery brush over a small area of the surface. Character B and Character C inspect with restrained natural motions. No sprinkles fly; product geometry remains fixed.

### Beat B — 00:02.5–00:06.5 — Assortment reveal
The same continuous camera move pulls back and rises slightly, revealing nearby pink, blue, purple, white and elongated-sprinkle truffles. The three chefs remain in the same local region; they do not teleport or run across the box. Their secondary gestures are subtle. The product collection becomes the visual subject.

### Beat C — 00:06.5–00:10.0 — Full-box hero
The camera continues the same smooth move until the open natural kraft box and full colorful assortment are readable in a premium 3/4 composition. Chef motion settles before the end. The last ~1 second is visually stable and product-dominant, suitable for later branding/text overlay in post-production.

## Product behavior
- all truffles already exist from the first frame;
- no product assembly/disassembly;
- no color change;
- no coating change;
- no melting;
- no deformation;
- no count changes;
- no hard-shell bonbon interpretation;
- paper cups remain attached to their respective truffles.

## Character behavior
Use exactly the three recurring chefs from selected `P0001-R0010`.
- Character A: one simple brush action only in Beat A.
- Character B: inspection / small approval gesture only.
- Character C: attentive inspection / restrained hand movement only.
- No walking long distances.
- No climbing.
- No ladders.
- No carts.
- No complex props.

## Camera philosophy
One coherent movement only: a slow continuous pull-back following a shallow upward arc. No cuts, whip pans, sudden zooms, orbit changes or reframing jumps.

## Final frame
Premium 3/4 view of the real kraft box filled with colorful handmade truffles against a dark neutral studio world. Product occupies the majority of visual attention. Tiny chefs remain visible as a delightful scale cue but do not compete with the product.

## Risk level
Low-to-medium relative to process-heavy alternatives.

Primary remaining risks:
- character identity drift during scale change;
- product over-regularization;
- accidental duplication of chefs/truffles;
- brush/hand contact artifacts;
- camera pull-back causing background morphing.

These risks must be explicitly handled in scene-keyframe and final video prompts.

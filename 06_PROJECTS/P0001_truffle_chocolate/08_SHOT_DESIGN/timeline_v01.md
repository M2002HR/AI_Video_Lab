# P0001 Shot Timeline v01

## Format
- Duration: exactly 10 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Shot count: one continuous shot
- Camera move: one continuous slow pull-back on a shallow upward arc
- Visual language: premium dark-studio macro miniature food commercial

## Scale lock
For v1 scene design, target chef height at roughly **35–45% of one truffle diameter**. This is a creative scale cue, not a measurement of the real product. It should make one truffle feel large enough to be a miniature-worksite object while keeping chefs readable in the starting macro frame.

Do not let chef scale change during the clip.

## 00:00–00:00.8 — Establish
- Very close 3/4 macro on one multicolor round-nonpareil hero truffle in its dark fluted paper cup.
- Three recurring tiny chocolatiers occupy a small coherent area around the lower/near side of the truffle.
- Background dark and minimal.
- Product already complete; no assembly begins.

## 00:00.8–00:02.5 — Final-touch action
- Character A performs one short controlled brush pass over a small visible region of the truffle coating.
- Brush remains in continuous physical contact during the pass.
- No particles detach/fly.
- Character B performs one small inspection/approval gesture.
- Character C observes with restrained motion.
- Camera begins barely perceptible backward/upward travel.

## 00:02.5–00:04.5 — Nearby diversity appears
- Camera pull-back becomes more apparent but remains smooth.
- 3–4 neighboring truffles enter frame naturally.
- Distinct coating/color families become visible.
- Characters remain in their local region; no teleporting/walking across frame.
- Brush action is finished; tool lowers and becomes secondary.

## 00:04.5–00:06.5 — Assortment expands
- More of the truffle collection and dark cups becomes visible.
- Kraft packaging begins to read around the group.
- Camera does not change direction.
- Character motion becomes minimal.
- No object count changes.

## 00:06.5–00:08.8 — Full-box reveal
- Camera reaches premium 3/4 full-box framing.
- Natural kraft box, colorful assortment, dark cups and handmade character are all readable.
- Tiny chefs remain visible as scale accents around the original hero-truffle region.
- Product becomes dominant.

## 00:08.8–00:10.0 — Stable hero hold
- Camera eases to near-stop.
- Secondary character/tool motion settles.
- Keep final frame visually stable for post-production branding/text.
- No new action begins in the last second.

## Continuity rules
- exactly three chefs throughout;
- same faces, hats, jackets, trousers and shoes;
- no extra or missing limbs;
- no character duplication;
- no truffle duplication/disappearance;
- coating pattern does not morph between families;
- kraft box geometry remains stable;
- all paper cups remain dark fluted paper;
- hero truffle never becomes a cake pop, bonbon shell, cupcake or generic candy ball;
- no cheesecake/cherry contamination;
- same lighting direction throughout;
- camera path continuous and physically plausible.

## Audio direction (optional later)
If audio is generated, keep it restrained: soft miniature brush texture, subtle paper/fabric movement, faint premium studio ambience. No dialogue is required for v1.

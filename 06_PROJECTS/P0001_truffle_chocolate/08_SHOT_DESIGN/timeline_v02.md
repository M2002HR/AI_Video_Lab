# P0001 Shot Timeline v02 — Quiet Inspection → Full Box Reveal

## Format
- Duration: exactly 10 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Shot count: one continuous shot
- Camera: one slow pull-back with shallow upward rise
- Scene: all action occurs inside the same real kraft box from frame one
- Props/tools: none

## Scale lock
Hero truffle diameter ≈ **3× one chef's full standing height**. All three chefs use the same miniature scale and scale remains constant throughout the shot.

## 00:00–00:00.8 — Establish inside the box
- Close 3/4 macro on one multicolor round-nonpareil hero truffle in its dark fluted cup.
- A small visible area of the natural kraft box base/edge and partial neighboring cups proves this is already inside the final product box.
- Exactly three recurring chefs are positioned in front/near-side gaps between cups; all three faces readable in 3/4 view.
- Hands empty.

## 00:00.8–00:02.2 — Quiet inspection
- Character A makes a slight head/upper-body inspection lean.
- Character B makes one small open-hand approval/inspection gesture without touching or modifying the truffle.
- Character C performs a restrained nod or small hand motion.
- Product remains perfectly stationary.
- Camera begins an almost imperceptible backward/upward move.

## 00:02.2–00:04.3 — Neighboring truffles enter frame
- Smooth camera pull-back reveals 2–4 neighboring truffles already present in their paper cups.
- Supported colors/coating families appear naturally.
- Characters stay in the same local region.
- No new objects appear.

## 00:04.3–00:06.2 — Box structure becomes readable
- More existing assortment enters frame.
- Natural kraft box interior/edges become clearly understandable.
- Camera direction remains unchanged.
- Character gestures settle further.

## 00:06.2–00:09.0 — Full-box hero reveal
- Camera reaches a premium 3/4 full-box composition.
- Colorful handmade assortment, dark paper cups and kraft box are the dominant subject.
- Chefs remain visible but small.
- No truffle count changes or appearance/disappearance.

## 00:09.0–00:10.0 — Stable hold
- Camera eases nearly to a stop.
- Character motion is minimal to none.
- Final frame remains clean for post-production logo/text.

## Hard continuity rules
- exactly three recurring chefs throughout;
- same chef scale, faces, hats, red jackets, trousers and shoes;
- empty hands; no tools/props introduced;
- all truffles exist from frame one outside the close crop; camera reveals them rather than generating them;
- hero truffle stays in the same cup and physical location;
- no loose/flying nonpareils;
- no wooden workbench scene;
- no isolated platform separate from box;
- no box transformation;
- no product morphing, duplication or disappearing;
- handmade truffle geometry must not become perfect CGI spheres;
- same lighting direction and one physically plausible camera path.

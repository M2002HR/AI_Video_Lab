# P0001 Storyboard Plan v01 — Final Touch → Full Box Reveal

This is a planning storyboard for one continuous 10-second shot. Generate frames separately; do not create a multi-panel collage for production reference use.

## SB/KF01 — Opening macro, ~00:01.0
Purpose: lock chef-to-truffle scale, initial composition and premium miniature scene language.

Composition:
- one multicolor round-nonpareil truffle dominates the frame in its dark fluted cup;
- exactly three recurring chefs from R0010 gather along the near/lower side;
- Character A holds one tiny soft confectionery brush near the truffle surface;
- Characters B/C inspect; no other tools;
- only small hints of neighboring truffles/cups may appear at frame edges;
- dark neutral studio world;
- close 3/4 macro camera.

Critical locks:
- chef height approximately 35–45% of truffle diameter;
- truffle remains handmade, not a perfect sphere;
- particles remain tiny edible nonpareils;
- no old cheesecake contamination.

## SB/KF02 — Mid assortment reveal, ~00:05.0
Purpose: verify continuity when more product diversity enters frame.

Composition:
- original hero truffle remains identifiable;
- 5–8 nearby assorted truffles visible, including round nonpareils and elongated sprinkles;
- three chefs remain clustered near original hero region at identical scale;
- kraft box begins to become visible;
- camera is farther back but on same visual axis/path;
- no object/character duplication.

## SB/KF03 — Final hero, ~00:09.2
Purpose: lock end-state commercial composition.

Composition:
- complete open natural kraft box readable in premium 3/4 view;
- colorful handmade assortment dominates;
- three tiny chefs remain visible near original hero-truffle region as scale accents;
- character motion implied settled;
- dark studio background;
- stable, clean negative space for optional post-production branding;
- no text generated in-frame.

## Continuity relationship
KF01 → KF02 → KF03 must describe the SAME box, SAME product family, SAME three chefs, SAME lighting direction and SAME continuous camera pull-back/upward arc. No scene cut, no time jump, no transformation.

## Generation policy
Generate and QA KF01 first. If product/character scale or combined-scene identity fails, fix the combined-scene prompt before generating KF02/KF03. This prevents propagation of a bad scene grammar.

# P0001 Product Identity v1

## Category
Handmade chocolate truffles. They are not cake pops, cupcakes, cookies, macarons, donuts, hard-shell geometric bonbons or generic candy balls.

## Primary geometry
- Individual truffles are small, near-spherical handmade chocolate forms.
- Shapes are organic and slightly imperfect rather than mathematically identical spheres.
- The visible assortment contains multiple individual truffles rather than one monolithic dessert.

## Surface and decoration
- Dark chocolate body beneath the decoration.
- Dense decorative coating attached directly to the truffle surface.
- Two dominant coating families: tiny round sugar nonpareils and thin elongated sprinkles.
- Colorways visible in the source include white, pink, bright/dark blue, purple, yellow/lime, red-green-white, orange/brown and mixed rainbow combinations.
- Decorative particles must read as small edible pieces with natural shadows, not glossy plastic beads.

## Individual holder
Every truffle sits in its own small dark gray/charcoal fluted paper candy cup. The cup is part of the product presentation and must not silently become foil, plastic or a cupcake wrapper.

## Packaging
- Open natural kraft-brown cardboard gift box.
- Simple unbranded structure.
- Dense assortment arranged closely inside the box.
- The source photo's external wooden tabletop and `CHOCOLAT.CHOCOLATE` watermark are not identity traits.

## Material behavior
- Chocolate: dark, matte-to-satin food material with soft realistic highlights, not plastic.
- Sprinkles/nonpareils: dry small edible particles with slight irregularity and individual micro-shadows.
- Paper cups: matte folded paper with visible fluting.
- Kraft box: natural paperboard fibers, matte surface, warm brown tone.

## Identity-critical traits
1. truffle category;
2. near-spherical handmade form;
3. colorful dense sprinkle/nonpareil coatings;
4. dark fluted individual paper cups;
5. open natural kraft box;
6. multi-color assortment rather than a single uniform colorway;
7. handmade/artisanal imperfection;
8. no invented branding.

## Allowed variation
- exact individual sprinkle placement;
- tiny natural asymmetries in truffle shape;
- exact assortment order if the shot does not depend on matching the original top-down layout pixel-for-pixel;
- lighting/reflections appropriate to the new studio scene.

## Forbidden variation
- sticks, handles or cake-pop geometry;
- geometric hard bonbon shells;
- glossy toy/plastic appearance;
- missing paper cups in hero presentation;
- changing kraft packaging into luxury plastic/metal/velvet packaging;
- replacing colorful coatings with plain ganache unless the action explicitly concerns an unfinished intermediate truffle and the final product returns to the source identity;
- generated logos, labels or watermark text.

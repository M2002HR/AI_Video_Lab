```text
PRODUCT IDENTITY AUTHORITY
The original real product photograph is the highest-authority identity source. Generated reference views are supporting evidence only and must never override a visible trait from the original source.

The product is an artisanal assortment of handmade chocolate truffles. Preserve the exact category and overall visual language: small near-spherical dark-chocolate truffles densely coated directly with tiny edible round nonpareils or thin elongated edible sprinkles; each truffle sits in an individual dark charcoal-gray fluted matte paper candy cup; the assortment sits closely together inside a simple open natural kraft-brown cardboard box.

ARTISAN IMPERFECTION — CRITICAL
Do not regularize the product into a factory-perfect assortment. Preserve believable handmade variation. Individual truffles may differ subtly in diameter, roundness, coating density, particle distribution, surface contour and seating angle. They should belong to the same product family without becoming cloned identical spheres. The arrangement should feel hand-packed, not mathematically gridded or procedurally repeated.

The source product contains several visually distinct coating/color families including white, pink, bright/dark blue, purple, yellow/lime, red-green-white, orange/brown and mixed rainbow combinations. Preserve both round-nonpareil and elongated-sprinkle families. Do not collapse the assortment into one coating type or invent unrelated decoration styles.

MATERIALS
Chocolate is edible dark chocolate with restrained matte-to-satin food reflections. Decorations are tiny dry edible particles with irregular placement and micro-contact shadows, never large plastic beads. Cups are folded matte charcoal paper with visible fluting, not black plastic or foil. The box is simple warm kraft paperboard, not a thick luxury presentation case.

PACKAGING CONSERVATISM
Do not redesign the box. Preserve a shallow, simple, handmade kraft container. Do not thicken walls unnecessarily, add a lid/hinge/insert, ribbons, plastic windows, branding or luxury finishing unsupported by the source.

FORBIDDEN TRANSFORMATIONS
No cake pops, sticks, cupcakes, cookies, macarons, donuts, chocolate bars, hard-shell geometric bonbons, glossy toy candy balls, invented logos, invented labels or source watermark reproduction.

When hidden geometry is not visible in the source, infer minimally and conservatively. Product identity fidelity and handmade realism have priority over symmetry, visual perfection and aesthetic novelty.
```

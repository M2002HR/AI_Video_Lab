# Scenario Capacity Assessment — P0002

## Fixed target
30 seconds total = 3×10s. The task is to assess whether this duration has three genuinely distinct responsibilities and what architecture best balances commercial value and generative reliability.

## Capacity dimensions

### Product process richness — MEDIUM
The repository verifies several strong finished/presentation states but does not verify the literal manufacturing process. This blocks factual origin/making chains, yet still leaves meaningful creative-metaphor craft, inspection, arrangement and packaging/presentation chapters.

### Visual diversity — HIGH
The product offers multiple coating families, strong color variation, macro edible particle texture, handmade geometry, dark fluted cups, kraft packaging, individual-vs-assortment scale and a premium macro studio world.

### World / character capacity — HIGH
P0001 established a recurring trio of miniature red-uniform chocolatiers and a clean oversized-product world. Characters can support care, inspection, coordination and scale storytelling without needing to claim factual production.

### Reference readiness — MEDIUM-HIGH for planning, MEDIUM for production
Documented role authorities exist: R0003 clean identity, R0006 macro material, R0008 assortment diversity, R0010 character identity, R0015 scene master and P0001 keyframes. Binary media must be re-attached only after scenario selection and only where role-required.

### Generative feasibility — MEDIUM
P0001 proved that clean/minimal reference stacks and scene-master-derived camera states can work well, but also exposed three major risks: artisan regularization, reference competition/reconstruction and stochastic character duplication during off-screen reveal. Multi-clip hybrid/editorial boundaries reduce the need for fragile 30-second spatial continuity.

### Commercial arc — HIGH
30s provides real added value if the clips escalate rather than repeat: a tactile/craft hook, a collection/packaging development, then an unmistakable product hero payoff. Three editorial chapters are also viable because the assortment has enough visual diversity.

## Duration viability context
Although duration is fixed by the user, the system assessment is:

| Duration | Fit | Why | Meaningful scenario count |
|---|---|---|---:|
| 10s | `strong_fit` | Proven hero/inspection/reveal format | 2–3 |
| 20s | `strong_fit` | One craft/collection chapter plus payoff | 3–4 |
| 30s | `strong_fit` | Three distinct responsibilities are available without filler | 4 |
| 40s | `possible_but_low_value` | A fourth chapter would likely force unverified process or repeat collection/hero logic | 2–3 at most |

## Architecture recommendation
Default preference: `hybrid`.
- Hard continuity inside each 10s clip.
- Controlled match/editorial boundaries between clips.
- Shared identity/style bible but per-clip minimum-sufficient reference stacks.

`continuous_world` is possible but not recommended as the default because P0001 showed stochastic population failure during expanding reveals and scene reconstruction pressure when strong references compete.

## Candidate budget decision
Four candidates are retained. A fifth candidate was omitted because it would either be a superficial reorder of these families or require inventing a factual truffle-making process unsupported by repository evidence.

# Scenario Architecture Menu — P0002 / 30s / 3×10s

Selection gate: no heavy storyboard, keyframe or full image/video prompts before user selects one architecture.

## S30-A — Artisan Care → Collection Build → Gift-Box Hero
- Duration: 30s / 3×10s
- Architecture: `hybrid`
- Premise: Tiny chocolatiers treat the already-finished truffles as oversized artisanal objects of care; the story escalates from one tactile hero to an assembled collection and then to the full kraft-box commercial payoff.
- Process status: character actions and assembly are `creative_metaphor`; finished product/assortment/box states are visually verified.
- Clip map:
  - C01 — Macro Care: one finished hero truffle, three recurring chocolatiers inspect/coordinate around coating texture and handmade form; no claim of literal manufacturing.
  - C02 — Collection Build: multiple finished truffles/cups come into a deliberate assortment/arrangement chapter via controlled placement/match-cut logic.
  - C03 — Presentation Hero: full open kraft box, restrained character presence, premium reveal/hold focused on colorful assortment and handmade identity.
- Process depth: Medium
- Visual impact: High
- Generation risk: Medium
- New reference burden: Medium
- Strongest commercial advantage: strongest balance of tactile craftsmanship, miniature-world DNA, assortment breadth and clear final product payoff.
- Main failure risk: too many simultaneous placement interactions could create object/character duplication; keep C02 actions sparse and boundary-driven.

## S30-B — One Truffle → Color Collection → Packed Presentation
- Duration: 30s / 3×10s
- Architecture: `hybrid`
- Premise: Start with a single finished truffle as the identity anchor, expand through the product’s real color/coating diversity, then resolve into the verified kraft-box presentation.
- Process status: no manufacturing claim; all product states are finished-state/product-presentation based. Any movement into cups/box is `creative_metaphor` unless later user-confirmed.
- Clip map:
  - C01 — One Item: extreme macro study of one handmade truffle in its dark fluted cup; tiny chocolatiers provide scale and inspection rather than making.
  - C02 — Collection: editorial/match-cut expansion across several coating/color families, with characters optional or reduced to avoid count pressure.
  - C03 — Packaging Payoff: open kraft box assortment becomes the dominant hero; clean premium camera movement and final hold.
- Process depth: Low–Medium
- Visual impact: High
- Generation risk: Low–Medium
- New reference burden: Low–Medium
- Strongest commercial advantage: communicates actual product variety and packaging truth with less speculative action and lower continuity burden.
- Main failure risk: can feel like three beautiful reveals rather than a story if C02 does not add strong escalation/rhythm.

## S30-C — Three Editorial Worlds: Texture / Color / Scale
- Duration: 30s / 3×10s
- Architecture: `editorial_sequence`
- Premise: Three independent premium chapters each sell a different product truth—edible texture, assortment/color diversity, and miniature-scale gift-box spectacle—joined by rhythm, lighting grammar and match cuts rather than spatial continuity.
- Process status: primarily verified finished-state storytelling; character-scale actions are `creative_metaphor`.
- Clip map:
  - C01 — Texture World: macro coating particles, chocolate material, cup folds, handmade irregularity; minimal or no characters.
  - C02 — Color World: curated assortment of nonpareil and elongated-sprinkle families, editorial transitions between distinct truffles.
  - C03 — Scale World: tiny recurring chocolatiers inside/around the open kraft-box world, ending on the full commercial hero.
- Process depth: Low
- Visual impact: Very High
- Generation risk: Low–Medium
- New reference burden: Medium (different chapter-specific role stacks)
- Strongest commercial advantage: highest visual polish/reliability potential for AI because each clip can optimize one product truth independently.
- Main failure risk: weaker narrative/emotional continuity; depends on edit/audio/match concepts to feel like one 30s ad rather than a montage.

## S30-D — Chocolatier Mission: Inspect → Organize → Present
- Duration: 30s / 3×10s
- Architecture: `hybrid` (character-driven)
- Premise: The recurring three-chef cast carries the sequence: they inspect a hero truffle, coordinate the finished assortment, then step back to present the completed gift-box world.
- Process status: all character labor is `creative_metaphor`; the finished product and final packaging states are verified.
- Clip map:
  - C01 — Inspect: exact recurring trio around one oversized finished truffle, each with a readable but simple inspection role.
  - C02 — Organize: trio coordinates around a larger finished assortment; movement/action is limited to preserve count and identity.
  - C03 — Present: same trio acts as scale/host figures while the box becomes the hero; final composition prioritizes product over characters.
- Process depth: Medium
- Visual impact: High
- Generation risk: Medium–High
- New reference burden: Medium
- Strongest commercial advantage: strongest branded-character memory and emotional continuity while preserving the successful miniature chocolatier DNA.
- Main failure risk: exact trio count/identity across three clips is the largest burden; P0001 R0023 proves off-screen reveal can instantiate an extra chef, so boundaries should avoid uncontrolled spatial expansion.

## Recommendation
Default recommendation: **S30-A** for the best commercial balance. It gives each clip a distinct job and uses the miniature chocolatier concept as a craft metaphor without pretending to document a real production recipe.

Safest production-oriented alternative: **S30-C**. It reduces cross-clip spatial/count continuity and lets product identity dominate each chapter.

## Omitted families
- Literal origin/ingredient-to-product: omitted because real process is not verified.
- Full continuous 30s factory/worksite: omitted because it combines the highest character/object continuity burden with unsupported process claims.
- Simple reveal repeated three times: omitted as filler/duplicate architecture.

No repository evidence confirms the literal manufacturing recipe or factory process for these truffles. Therefore no forming, dipping, coating, curing, filling or wrapping step may be presented as factual production unless the user later confirms it or supplies evidence.

## Safe 30s decomposition opportunities
1. `care/craft metaphor → collection/assembly metaphor → verified hero presentation`.
2. `individual finished product → assortment diversity → verified kraft-box hero` without claiming manufacturing.
3. Three editorial product chapters using verified finished states, material detail and character world.
4. Character-driven inspection journey in which the tiny chocolatiers interact with already-finished product states.

## Unsafe / omitted without new evidence
- recipe/origin story;
- melting chocolate, filling centers, tempering, dipping, curing or exact coating machinery presented as real;
- ingredient-to-product transformation framed as documentary truth.

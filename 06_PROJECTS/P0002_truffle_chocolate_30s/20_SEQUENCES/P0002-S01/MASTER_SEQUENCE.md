# MASTER SEQUENCE — P0002-S01

## Sequence
- Project: `P0002`
- Parent project: `P0001`
- Selected scenario: `S30-A — Artisan Care → Collection Build → Gift-Box Hero`
- Total duration: 30s
- Clip count: 3
- Clip duration: 10s each
- Architecture mode: `hybrid`
- Target delivery: 16:9
- Target tool/model inherited from project: Google Flow / Gemini Omni Flash

## Overall commercial arc
`Tactile handmade hero → expanding colorful collection → unmistakable gift-box payoff`

The sequence should increase product breadth and commercial clarity each chapter. It must not feel like three independent reveals, and it must not pretend to document the literal manufacturing recipe.

## Shared identity bible
Across all clips preserve:
- handmade near-spherical truffle geometry with believable irregularity;
- dark chocolate beneath dense edible coating;
- both nonpareil and elongated-sprinkle coating families;
- broad source-supported color diversity;
- dark charcoal fluted matte paper cups;
- shallow simple open natural kraft-brown box;
- premium macro dark-studio grammar;
- no branding/text/watermark generation;
- miniature red-uniform chocolatier identity when characters appear;
- exact recurring trio only; no additional humans/chefs anywhere in the world.

## Clip roles
### P0002-C01 — Artisan Care / Macro Hook
Commercial job: make the product feel tactile, handmade and precious before showing the full assortment.
Start product state: one finished decorated truffle in its cup, macro-dominant.
Character state: exact recurring trio, simple readable inspection/care positions.
Action scope: one principal inspection/care idea plus restrained camera development.
End state: hero truffle remains intact and traceable; composition prepared for a shape/color/action match into C02.

### P0002-C02 — Collection Build
Commercial job: expand from one-item craft perception to the breadth, color and abundance of the finished assortment.
Start product state: several finished truffles/cups already present; no raw or unfinished manufacturing claim.
Character state: exact trio may coordinate sparse placement/organization; no crowd expansion.
Action scope: one controlled assortment-building/arrangement action; avoid many simultaneous carries/placements.
End state: a coherent partial/full collection pattern that supports a composition match into the final box chapter.

### P0002-C03 — Gift-Box Hero
Commercial job: resolve all preceding craft/color energy into the verified product presentation and final sales image.
Start product state: finished assortment already seated in dark cups inside the open kraft box.
Character state: exact trio, visually secondary, used for scale/presentation only.
Action scope: controlled premium reveal/settle; no new product-building process.
End state: stable open-box hero hold with product clearly dominant.

## Boundary C01 → C02
Transition type: `match/action cut`, not hard spatial continuity.
Exact continuity required:
- same product family and handmade identity;
- coating/material scale remains believable;
- same global lighting/color grammar;
- recurring characters, if visible on both sides, remain the same exact trio.
Allowed discontinuity:
- camera angle/distance;
- exact truffle spatial placement;
- environment micro-layout.
Preferred bridge: hero truffle shape/color/motion direction motivates the cut into a larger collection state.
Reason: reduces reconstruction pressure while preserving narrative escalation.

## Boundary C02 → C03
Transition type: `composition/match cut` or controlled editorial cut.
Exact continuity required:
- finished assortment state;
- correct cups and kraft packaging identity;
- same coating/color family language;
- exact trio only if characters persist across the boundary.
Allowed discontinuity:
- precise arrangement geometry;
- camera relocation to final commercial angle;
- partial-to-full box expansion through edit rather than morph.
Preferred bridge: pattern/row/box-edge composition match into the full gift-box hero.
Reason: avoid a long generative zoom-out that could instantiate extra characters or reconstruct the assortment.

## Audio / edit continuity direction
Not yet a final audio plan. Working principle: one escalating premium rhythm across all three clips, with transition accents at 10s and 20s and a calmer final hero hold. No generated on-screen copy is required at this stage.

## Final payoff
By the final seconds, the viewer should understand: artisanal handmade texture + colorful assortment breadth + gift-ready kraft-box presentation, with miniature chocolatiers functioning as memorable scale/craft metaphor rather than the product itself.

## Accepted discontinuities
Because architecture is hybrid, pixel-perfect spatial continuity across clips is not required. Product identity, material truth, cast identity/count, scale grammar and commercial escalation are required.

# P0002 Product Identity v1

P0002 uses the same physical product family as P0001. This record is re-established from the current uploaded product photograph and is consistent with the validated P0001 identity documentation.

## Category
Artisanal handmade chocolate truffles, presented as a colorful assortment in an open kraft gift box.

## Shape and variation
- small near-spherical chocolate truffles;
- slight natural asymmetry and handmade variation;
- never cloned factory-perfect spheres;
- multiple separate pieces, not one cake or monolithic dessert.

## Surface decoration
- dense edible coatings attached directly to the truffle surface;
- both tiny round nonpareils and thin elongated sprinkles;
- visible color families include white, pink, bright/dark blue, purple, yellow/lime, red-green-white, orange/brown and mixed rainbow combinations;
- particles read as dry edible decoration with micro-shadows, not plastic beads.

## Individual holders
Each finished truffle sits in a dark charcoal-gray fluted matte paper candy cup.

## Packaging
- shallow, simple, open natural kraft-brown cardboard gift box;
- unbranded handmade presentation;
- densely packed assortment;
- the source wooden tabletop and watermark are not part of product identity.

## Material behavior
- chocolate: dark edible material with restrained matte-to-satin highlights;
- coatings: dry, tiny, irregular edible particles;
- cups: folded matte paper with visible fluting;
- box: warm matte kraft paperboard.

## Forbidden transformations
No cake pops or sticks, cupcakes, cookies, macarons, donuts, hard geometric bonbons, glossy toy candy, brownies, cake slices, invented logos, invented labels, ribbons, plastic windows or watermark reproduction.

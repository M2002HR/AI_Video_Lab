# Selected Scenario v01 — Invisible-Chef Truffle Assembly

## Concept
A fixed 90-degree top-down premium food-commercial shot shows an invisible chef creating the truffles and assembling the recognizable colorful kraft-box assortment. The entire process occurs on a pure white tabletop with no visible person and no camera movement.

## Beat architecture
1. **Packaging setup** — the kraft box and dark fluted candy cups self-position in the exact centered overhead composition.
2. **Chocolate formation** — dark chocolate and cream form glossy ganache; the mixture portions itself into subtly irregular handmade truffle centers.
3. **Color transformation** — nonpareils and elongated sprinkles coat the truffles in the source-matched color families.
4. **Box assembly** — finished truffles transfer into the individual cups and settle with believable weight and collisions until the box matches the product reference.
5. **Hero payoff** — the complete box holds cleanly; one multicolor truffle lifts, receives a realistic bite, sheds a few crumbs/sprinkles and remains floating at the end.

## Locked camera and scene policy
- exactly 90-degree overhead;
- camera fully fixed for all 10 seconds;
- 16:9;
- pure white tabletop;
- centered frame;
- soft diffused studio light;
- no visible hands, arms, people or bodies;
- no unrelated props;
- old miniature-chef concept is not used in this derivative.

## Product lock
The final boxed assortment must remain recognizably the uploaded handmade truffle product. The process is subordinate to identity fidelity.

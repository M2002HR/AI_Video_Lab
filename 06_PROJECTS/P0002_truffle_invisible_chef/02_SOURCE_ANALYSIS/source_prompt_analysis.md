# P0002 Source Prompt Analysis v1

## Reusable creative DNA
- strict 10-second timeline;
- fixed 90-degree overhead camera;
- premium ultra-realistic food-commercial aesthetic;
- white minimalist tabletop and centered composition;
- invisible-chef object animation with no visible human body parts;
- realistic physical contact, gravity, liquids and food motion;
- rapid ingredient-to-finished-product transformation;
- final product presentation followed by one lifted piece and a bite gag.

## KEEP
- duration: 10 seconds;
- aspect ratio: 16:9;
- camera fixed exactly top-down for the full clip;
- soft diffused studio light;
- pure white clean background;
- centered workspace;
- invisible-chef rule;
- timecoded sequential actions;
- realistic physics language;
- final hero presentation;
- lifted-piece and realistic-bite ending.

## ADAPT
- brownie preparation -> handmade chocolate-truffle preparation and gift-box assembly;
- round cake pan -> shallow open kraft gift box plus dark fluted paper candy cups;
- brownie batter -> glossy dark-chocolate ganache that becomes individual truffle centers;
- flour/sieve/baking workflow -> shaping and coating workflow using the product's real nonpareil and elongated-sprinkle families;
- brownie slices on a white plate -> complete colorful truffle assortment seated in individual dark paper cups inside the kraft box;
- one lifted brownie slice -> one lifted multicolor truffle with a natural bite exposing dark chocolate interior;
- source composition instruction -> match the uploaded truffle product reference for box orientation, assortment density, packaging language and final presentation, while replacing the source wooden tabletop with the required white studio surface.

## REMOVE
- cake pan and cake geometry;
- butter/sugar/eggs/flour/cocoa/baking-powder steps that only describe brownie batter;
- baking/rising brownie transformation;
- knife and equal-slice cutting;
- serving plate and circular brownie-slice arrangement;
- any brownie-specific crumbs, surface or structure;
- any visible or generated watermark/text from the product photo.

## Risks and controls
1. The source template is unusually dense for 10 seconds. P0002 reduces the number of distinct utensil changes and groups related truffle-forming actions into larger beats.
2. The final assortment contains many separate pieces, which creates count/layout drift risk. The uploaded product image remains the final-presentation authority; no extra truffles may appear outside the box.
3. Perfectly spherical procedural truffles would violate product identity. The prompt explicitly requires subtle handmade irregularity.
4. Levitation can look magical rather than physical. Motion must retain acceleration, deceleration, collisions, settling and weight cues.
5. The source product photograph includes a wooden tabletop and watermark. Both are explicitly excluded from the generated scene.

## Adaptation rule
Preserve the source prompt's camera, invisible-chef mechanism, timing discipline and transformation grammar. Replace all brownie-specific content with identity-faithful truffle production and packaging behavior.

# OBS-0012 — Off-screen reveal can instantiate an extra character

Status: `observation`
Confidence: `provisional`
Project: `P0001`
Runs: `P0001-R0022`, `P0001-R0023`

## Observation
Two Google Flow / Gemini Omni Flash 10-second video generations used the same five uploaded ingredients, the same upload order, the same V01 prompt and the same exposed settings.

`R0022` maintained exactly three chefs for the full sampled timeline and produced a strong continuous reveal.

`R0023` began with the same intended three chefs, but as the camera pulled backward and revealed previously off-screen space, an additional red-uniform chef emerged at the far-right edge (partially visible around 2.4s, clearly present by approximately 3.0s) and remained through the final frame.

## Interpretation
Exact character-count constraints can fail stochastically during spatial reveal even when the visible scene anchors themselves contain exactly three characters and the prompt already says `exactly THREE` / `no fourth chef`.

The failure appears specifically associated with extrapolating newly visible off-screen world area, not with an initial-frame count error.

## Local action
Keep the current five-image ingredient stack constant for the next test and change only the prompt by adding an explicit OFF-SCREEN POPULATION LOCK:
- the three chefs visible at frame 0 are the only characters/humans anywhere in the entire world;
- zero chefs exist outside the initial crop;
- newly revealed space may contain only product, cups, kraft box and empty studio background;
- Ingredient 5 must identify the three already-present chefs and must never instantiate additional copies.

Generate two V02 runs before changing the ingredient stack.

## Promotion status
Do not promote this to a global rule yet. Validate on repeated runs and later projects first.
